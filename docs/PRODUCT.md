# Product

The frozen V1 product contract is [PRODUCT-DEFINITION-V1.md](PRODUCT-DEFINITION-V1.md). FlickSend is a
browser-first person-to-person handoff product for huge files and folders, not cloud payload storage,
sync, backup, a collaboration suite, or a file manager.

Its working tagline is **Send files like messages.** The name, trademark, domain, and legal status are
not cleared claims; P2 owns that work. The Engine Lab remains a validation environment, not the final
product UI.
