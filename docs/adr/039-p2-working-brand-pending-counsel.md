# ADR 039: Retain FlickSend Internally Pending Counsel Review

## Status

Accepted for P2 research closeout on 2026-09-10.

## Context

The P1 definition uses FlickSend as a working product name. P2 public research found no result in the exact
one-word USPTO `FLICKSEND` Wordmark query, but `flicksend.com` is registered, the `Flick` root is crowded,
and an Apple-listed `Flick.` product operates in the broadly similar file-sharing category. WIPO, EUIPO,
UK IPO, and IPO Pakistan have not produced repeatable official query results in P2.

## Decision

Use FlickSend only as an internal working brand under the P2 decision `CONTINUE_PENDING_COUNSEL`. Do not
start P3, publish launch branding, acquire a domain, register a trademark or company, create social accounts,
or make legal-clearance/exclusivity claims without explicit authorization and qualified counsel review.

## Consequences

- The P1 product definition and repository naming remain unchanged.
- P2 is complete as research, not as formal legal clearance.
- Counsel must review the relevant marks, classes, and target markets before public launch or material brand
  commitment.
- The documented conditional domain recommendation is not an acquisition instruction.
