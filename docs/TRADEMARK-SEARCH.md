## Risk Analysis

`Flick` is suggestive of a fast gesture or action for file transfer; `Send` is descriptive of the requested
action. The compound `FlickSend` is more distinctive than either component, but its strength may be limited
by the crowded root and descriptive suffix. The public `Flick.` transfer listing raises a `MEDIUM` product
risk based on name, phonetic, visual, and goods/services proximity. It is not enough to make a legal
conflict finding by itself. No similarity conclusion is made for unsearched WIPO, EUIPO, UK, or Pakistan
records.

4. Include common-law, company, domain, app-store, and social-use research for priority markets.
5. Make filing and geographic decisions only after the launch markets and legal entity strategy are known.
