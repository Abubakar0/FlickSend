# P2 Brand Decision

```text
P2 BRAND DECISION:
CONTINUE_PENDING_COUNSEL
```

FlickSend may continue as the internal working V1 brand. P2 found no immediate exact-name blocker in the
completed exact USPTO query or the limited public product searches. That is insufficient for a public launch:
`flicksend.com` is registered, the exact root `Flick` is commercially crowded, and an Apple-listed `Flick.`
file-sharing product has closely related transfer use. WIPO, EUIPO, UK IPO, and IPO Pakistan searches were
not completed with repeatable official results.

```text
FORMAL LEGAL / TRADEMARK CLEARANCE:
NOT ESTABLISHED BY P2
```

## Operating Boundary

- Continue internal development under the working brand only.
- Do not buy or negotiate for a domain, file a trademark, form a company, create brand accounts, or make
  public exclusivity/clearance claims without an explicit decision and qualified counsel advice.
- Do not automatically rename the codebase or change the frozen P1 definition.
- If a launch, filing, public campaign, or substantial brand investment is proposed, complete counsel review
  before that action.

## Counsel Priorities

1. Investigate `Flick.` by ydangle and live/pending `FLICK` records for relevant software, data-transmission,
   and SaaS services.
2. Complete exact, phrase, hyphenated, phonetic, visual, and common-law searches in the home operating
   jurisdiction and primary launch markets.
3. Prioritize United States if commercially targeted, then United Kingdom/EU and Pakistan where targeted;
   WIPO results may guide additional international review.
4. Determine the actual goods/services and appropriate Nice terms only after the final V1 offering, entity,
   and launch markets are established.
5. Recheck the chosen domain at authorized acquisition time; domain registration is not trademark clearance.

## Product/Entity Separation

The product brand, legal company/entity, domain, and any trademark registrations are separate decisions.
P2 approves none of them. It only permits the working product name to remain in internal development pending
professional review.
